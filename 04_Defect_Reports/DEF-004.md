## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in as a student.
2. Open /quiz?topic=Python&level=Expert123.
3. Answer the questions, click Submit answers and open Dashboard.

## Expected result
Only Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced is accepted.

## Actual result
The quiz ran and "Expert123" was saved and shown in the quiz history.
